# BNS 2023 — Chapter V: Offences against woman and child

## Section 63 — Rape (definition)
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 63 | topic: sexual_violence | formerly_ipc: 375
- keywords: rape, consent, forced sex, without consent, against will, intoxication, under 18
A man commits "rape" if he does any of the acts in clauses (a) to (d): (a) penetration of the vagina, mouth, urethra or anus with the penis, to any extent, or makes her do so with him or another person; (b) inserts any object or body part (not the penis) into the vagina, urethra or anus; (c) manipulates any part of a woman's body to cause penetration; (d) applies his mouth to the vagina, anus or urethra.
These acts are rape under any of seven descriptions: (i) against her will; (ii) without her consent; (iii) consent obtained by putting her or someone she cares about in fear of death or hurt; (iv) consent given because she believes he is another man to whom she is or believes herself lawfully married; (v) consent given while unable to understand the nature and consequences because of unsoundness of mind, intoxication, or a stupefying substance given by him or through another; (vi) with or without consent when she is under eighteen; (vii) when she is unable to communicate consent.
Explanation 2: consent means an unequivocal voluntary agreement communicated by words, gestures or any verbal or non-verbal communication of willingness for the specific sexual act. A woman who does not physically resist is not, for that reason alone, regarded as consenting.
Exception 1: a medical procedure is not rape. Exception 2: sexual intercourse by a man with his own wife, the wife not being under eighteen, is not rape.

## Section 64 — Punishment for rape
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 64 | topic: sexual_violence | formerly_ipc: 376
- keywords: punishment for rape, custodial rape, rape by police, rape by relative or teacher, repeated rape
64(1): rigorous imprisonment of not less than ten years, which may extend to imprisonment for life, and fine.
64(2): for aggravated cases, rigorous imprisonment of not less than ten years, extending to imprisonment for life (meaning the remainder of natural life), and fine. Aggravated cases include rape by: a police officer (in the police station limits, station house premises, or a woman in his custody); a public servant on a woman in his custody; armed forces member in a deployed area; staff of a jail, remand home or women's/children's institution on an inmate; staff of a hospital on a woman in that hospital; a relative, guardian, teacher or person in a position of trust or authority; rape during communal or sectarian violence; rape of a woman known to be pregnant; of a woman incapable of giving consent; by a person in a position of control or dominance; of a woman with mental or physical disability; rape causing grievous bodily harm, maiming, disfigurement or endangering life; and repeated rape of the same woman.

## Section 65 — Punishment for rape in certain cases (young victims)
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 65 | topic: sexual_violence_minor
65(1): rape of a woman under sixteen: rigorous imprisonment of not less than twenty years, extending to imprisonment for life (remainder of natural life), and fine. The fine must be just and reasonable to meet the victim's medical expenses and rehabilitation and is paid to the victim.
65(2): rape of a woman under twelve: rigorous imprisonment of not less than twenty years, extending to imprisonment for life (remainder of natural life), and fine, or death.

## Section 66 — Rape causing death or persistent vegetative state
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 66 | topic: sexual_violence
Whoever commits an offence under s.64(1) or 64(2) and in the course of it inflicts an injury causing the woman's death or a persistent vegetative state: rigorous imprisonment of not less than twenty years, extending to life (remainder of natural life), or death.

## Section 67 — Sexual intercourse by husband upon wife during separation
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 67 | topic: sexual_violence_marriage
- keywords: marital rape, husband, separated wife, without consent, decree of separation
A man who has sexual intercourse with his own wife, who is living separately (under a decree of separation or otherwise), without her consent: imprisonment of not less than two years, extending to seven years, and fine.

## Section 68 — Sexual intercourse by a person in authority
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 68 | topic: sexual_violence_abuse_of_authority
A person in a position of authority or fiduciary relationship, a public servant, a superintendent or manager of a jail, remand home or women's/children's institution, or a person on the management or staff of a hospital, who abuses that position to induce or seduce a woman in his custody, charge or present in the premises to have sexual intercourse with him (not amounting to rape): rigorous imprisonment of not less than five years, extending to ten years, and fine.

## Section 69 — Sexual intercourse by deceitful means or false promise of marriage
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 69 | topic: sexual_violence_deceit
- keywords: false promise of marriage, cheating, promise of job or promotion, hiding identity
Sexual intercourse with a woman by deceitful means, or by a promise to marry made without any intention of fulfilling it, not amounting to rape: imprisonment up to ten years and fine. "Deceitful means" includes inducement or false promise of employment or promotion, or marrying by suppressing identity.

## Section 70 — Gang rape
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 70 | topic: sexual_violence
70(1): where a woman is raped by one or more persons constituting a group or acting in furtherance of a common intention, each is deemed to have committed rape: rigorous imprisonment of not less than twenty years, extending to life (remainder of natural life), and fine; the fine is paid to the victim for medical expenses and rehabilitation.
70(2): where a woman under eighteen is gang raped: imprisonment for life (remainder of natural life) and fine, or death.

## Section 71 — Punishment for repeat offenders
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 71 | topic: sexual_violence
A person previously convicted under s.64, 65, 66 or 70 and convicted again under any of them: imprisonment for life (remainder of natural life), or death.

## Section 72 — Disclosure of identity of victim
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 72 | topic: privacy_victim_identity
- keywords: naming victim, publishing identity, media, social media post about victim
Printing or publishing the name or any matter that may make known the identity of a victim of an offence under ss.64 to 71: imprisonment up to two years and fine. Exceptions: by or under written order of the investigating police officer acting in good faith for investigation; by or with written authorisation of the victim; or, where the victim is dead, a child or of unsound mind, by or with written authorisation of the next of kin (only to the head or secretary of a recognised welfare institution).

## Section 73 — Printing or publishing court proceedings without permission
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 73 | topic: privacy_victim_identity
Printing or publishing any matter about court proceedings for an offence under s.72 without the court's previous permission: imprisonment up to two years and fine. Publication of a High Court or Supreme Court judgment is not an offence.

## Section 74 — Assault or criminal force to woman with intent to outrage her modesty
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 74 | topic: physical_harassment_assault | formerly_ipc: 354
- keywords: molestation, groping, touching inappropriately, outrage modesty, eve teasing, grabbed me, touched me, groped, crowded bus, metro, pinching
Whoever assaults or uses criminal force on any woman, intending to outrage or knowing it likely that he will thereby outrage her modesty: imprisonment of not less than one year, extending to five years, and fine.

## Section 75 — Sexual harassment
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 75 | topic: sexual_harassment | formerly_ipc: 354A
- keywords: sexual harassment, unwelcome advances, demand for sexual favours, pornography shown against will, sexually coloured remarks, comments, messages, workplace, public place, touching me, inappropriate messages, asking for favours, boss, colleague, lewd comments, porn shown to me
75(1): a man commits sexual harassment by any of: (i) physical contact and advances involving unwelcome and explicit sexual overtures; (ii) a demand or request for sexual favours; (iii) showing pornography against the will of a woman; (iv) making sexually coloured remarks.
75(2): for clause (i), (ii) or (iii): rigorous imprisonment up to three years, or fine, or both.
75(3): for clause (iv): imprisonment of either description up to one year, or fine, or both.

## Section 76 — Assault or criminal force to woman with intent to disrobe
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 76 | topic: physical_harassment_assault | formerly_ipc: 354B
Whoever assaults or uses criminal force on a woman, or abets it, with the intention of disrobing her or compelling her to be naked: imprisonment of not less than three years, extending to seven years, and fine.

## Section 77 — Voyeurism
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 77 | topic: privacy_online_abuse | formerly_ipc: 354C
- keywords: hidden camera, secretly recording, intimate photo, leaked video, sharing private images, changing room, bathroom, secretly filmed, spy camera, MMS, viral video, nude photos shared, revenge porn, leaked my pictures
Whoever watches, or captures the image of, a woman engaging in a private act in circumstances where she would usually expect not to be observed (by the perpetrator or anyone at his behest), or disseminates such an image: first conviction, imprisonment of not less than one year, extending to three years, and fine; second or subsequent conviction, not less than three years, extending to seven years, and fine.
Explanation 1: a "private act" includes watching in a place that would reasonably be expected to provide privacy where her genitals, posterior or breasts are exposed or covered only in underwear; where she is using a lavatory; or where she is doing a sexual act not ordinarily done in public.
Explanation 2: if she consents to the capture of images but not to their dissemination to third persons, and they are disseminated, that dissemination is an offence under this section.

## Section 78 — Stalking
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 78 | topic: stalking_online_harassment | formerly_ipc: 354D
- keywords: stalking, following, repeated calls, repeated messages, monitoring phone, tracking online activity, despite refusal, follows me, following me home, keeps texting me, messaging me, WhatsApp messages, calling repeatedly, won't leave me alone, colleague or stranger, metro, bus stop, street, tracking my location, checking my phone
78(1): a man commits stalking if he (i) follows a woman and contacts or attempts to contact her to foster personal interaction repeatedly despite a clear indication of disinterest by her; or (ii) monitors her use of the internet, e-mail or any other electronic communication.
Proviso: not stalking if he proves it was (i) to prevent or detect crime by a person entrusted with that responsibility by the State; (ii) under any law or to comply with a legal condition or requirement; or (iii) reasonable and justified in the circumstances.
78(2): first conviction, imprisonment up to three years and fine; second or subsequent conviction, up to five years and fine.

## Section 79 — Word, gesture or act intended to insult the modesty of a woman
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 79 | topic: verbal_harassment | formerly_ipc: 509
- keywords: eve teasing, catcalling, obscene gestures, whistling, lewd remarks, intruding on privacy, whistling, comments on the street, lewd gestures, obscene words, teasing
Whoever, intending to insult the modesty of any woman, utters any word, makes any sound or gesture, or exhibits any object, intending that it be heard or seen by her, or intrudes upon her privacy: simple imprisonment up to three years and fine.

## Section 80 — Dowry death
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 80 | topic: dowry_violence | formerly_ipc: 304B
- keywords: dowry death, bride burning, death within seven years of marriage, dowry harassment
80(1): where a woman's death is caused by burns or bodily injury, or occurs otherwise than under normal circumstances, within seven years of marriage, and it is shown that soon before her death she was subjected to cruelty or harassment by her husband or his relative for, or in connection with, a demand for dowry, it is called "dowry death" and the husband or relative is deemed to have caused her death. "Dowry" has the meaning in s.2 of the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961.
80(2): imprisonment of not less than seven years, extending to life.

## Section 81 — Cohabitation caused by deceitfully inducing belief of lawful marriage
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 81 | topic: marriage_deceit
A man who by deceit makes a woman not lawfully married to him believe she is lawfully married to him and cohabit or have sexual intercourse in that belief: imprisonment up to ten years and fine.

## Section 82 — Marrying again during lifetime of husband or wife (bigamy)
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 82 | topic: marriage_offences
82(1): marrying while a husband or wife is living, where the marriage is void for that reason: imprisonment up to seven years and fine (exceptions apply for marriages declared void by a court or a spouse continually absent and unheard of for seven years with disclosure). 82(2): if the former marriage is concealed from the new spouse: imprisonment up to ten years and fine.

## Section 85 — Husband or relative of husband subjecting woman to cruelty
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 85 | topic: domestic_cruelty_dowry_harassment | formerly_ipc: 498A
- keywords: 498A, cruelty by husband, cruelty by in-laws, dowry harassment, mental torture, domestic abuse
Whoever, being the husband or a relative of the husband of a woman, subjects her to cruelty: imprisonment up to three years and fine.

## Section 86 — Cruelty defined
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 86 | topic: domestic_cruelty_dowry_harassment | formerly_ipc: 498A (explanation)
For section 85, "cruelty" means: (a) any wilful conduct of such a nature as is likely to drive the woman to commit suicide or to cause grave injury or danger to life, limb or health (mental or physical); or (b) harassment of the woman with a view to coercing her or anyone related to her to meet any unlawful demand for property or valuable security, or on account of failure by her or anyone related to her to meet such demand.

## Section 87 — Kidnapping or abducting a woman to compel marriage or force illicit intercourse
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 87 | topic: abduction_forced_marriage | formerly_ipc: 366
Whoever kidnaps or abducts a woman intending that she be compelled to marry any person against her will, or forced or seduced to illicit intercourse (or knowing it likely): imprisonment up to ten years and fine. The same punishment applies to inducing a woman, by criminal intimidation, abuse of authority or any other compulsion, to go from any place intending or knowing it likely that she will be forced or seduced to illicit intercourse with another person.

# Related sections outside Chapter V

## Section 124 — Grievous hurt by use of acid
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 124 | chapter: Of hurt | topic: acid_attack | formerly_ipc: 326A, 326B
- keywords: acid attack, acid throwing, burns, disfigurement
124(1): causing permanent or partial damage or deformity, burns, maiming, disfigurement or disability, or grievous hurt by throwing or administering acid (or any other means) with that intention or knowledge, or causing a permanent vegetative state: imprisonment of not less than ten years, extending to life, and fine. The fine must be just and reasonable to meet the victim's treatment expenses and is paid to the victim.
124(2): throwing or attempting to throw acid, or attempting to administer acid or use other means, with the intention of causing such damage: imprisonment of not less than five years, extending to seven years, and fine.
"Acid" includes any substance with acidic or corrosive character or burning nature capable of causing scars, disfigurement or disability. Damage need not be irreversible.

## Section 351 — Criminal intimidation
- act: BNS 2023 | section: 351 | chapter: Of criminal intimidation | topic: threats_blackmail | formerly_ipc: 503, 506
- keywords: threats, blackmail, threatening to leak photos, threatening to defame, threatening to harm, anonymous threat, impute unchastity, blackmailing me, threatening to post my photos, threatening me, sextortion, threatening to ruin my reputation
351(1): threatening another by any means with injury to person, reputation or property (or to someone they are interested in), with intent to cause alarm or to make them do an act they are not legally bound to do, or omit one they are entitled to do, is criminal intimidation. A threat to injure the reputation of a deceased person in whom the threatened person is interested is covered.
351(2): imprisonment up to two years, or fine, or both.
351(3): if the threat is to cause death or grievous hurt, destroy property by fire, cause an offence punishable with death, life or up to seven years' imprisonment, or to impute unchastity to a woman: imprisonment up to seven years, or fine, or both.
351(4): intimidation by anonymous communication or concealing the sender's identity: imprisonment up to two years in addition to the punishment under 351(1).
