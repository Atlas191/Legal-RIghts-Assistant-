# Helplines and portals — who to contact for what

## Emergency — 112
- topic: emergency_helpline | status: supplementary, verify on official site | keywords: emergency, in danger now, police help, 112, call police, immediate help, ERSS, urgent
112 is the single all-India emergency number for police, fire and ambulance. Call it when there is immediate danger, including violence happening now. It works as the first step before filing any complaint.

## Women Helpline — 181 and One Stop Centres
- topic: women_helpline | status: supplementary, verify on official site | keywords: 181, women helpline, domestic violence helpline, One Stop Centre, Sakhi, shelter, counselling, police and medical help, 1091
181 is the Women Helpline run under the Ministry of Women and Child Development, with State-level variations. It connects a woman in distress to police, medical help, counselling and legal aid. One Stop Centres (also called Sakhi centres) give women affected by violence shelter, police help, medical aid, legal aid and counselling under one roof, and the helpline refers women to them. Some States also run 1091 as a police women's helpline.

## Online abuse, voyeurism and cybercrime — cybercrime.gov.in
- topic: cybercrime_reporting | status: supplementary, verify on official site | keywords: cybercrime portal, report online harassment, leaked photos, obscene messages, morphed pictures, stalking online, fake profile, sextortion, cybercrime.gov.in, 1930
The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) accepts complaints about online offences, including a section for crimes against women and children where the complainant can choose to report without giving her identity. Relevant laws include IT Act sections 66E, 67 and 67A, and BNS sections 77, 78 and 351. The number 1930 is the helpline for cyber financial fraud, not for online harassment, so a woman reporting harassment should use the portal or the police.

## Workplace sexual harassment — SHe-Box portal
- topic: workplace_complaint_portal | status: supplementary, verify on official site | keywords: SHe-Box, online complaint POSH, workplace harassment complaint portal, ICC online, shebox.wcd.gov.in, government portal
SHe-Box is the Government of India's online portal for registering complaints of workplace sexual harassment. A complaint made there is sent to the Internal Committee or Local Committee of the concerned workplace. The time limits and procedure under the POSH Act still apply. For the committees and timelines, see the posh_handbook and posh_act_supplement files.

## Free legal aid helpline — 15100
- topic: legal_aid_helpline | status: supplementary, verify on official site | keywords: 15100, legal aid helpline, NALSA helpline, free lawyer number, legal advice phone, DLSA contact
15100 is the National Legal Services Authority's toll-free helpline for legal aid and advice. It tells a caller how to reach the District Legal Services Authority and apply for a free lawyer. For eligibility and the application process, see the nalsa_charter file.

## National Commission for Women — grievances
- topic: ncw_contact | status: supplementary, verify on official site | keywords: NCW complaint, National Commission for Women contact, write to NCW, ncw.gov.in, grievance
Complaints can be made to the National Commission for Women through its website (ncw.gov.in) or by writing to it. The full contact details of the Public Grievance Officer, and the limits of what NCW can do, are in the ncw_charter file. NCW is not an implementing agency and takes matters up with the relevant authorities.

## Quick reference — which contact fits which situation
- source: Helplines and portals file | topic: contact_comparison | status: derived summary | keywords: who do I call, which number, where to report, what number do I call for help, helpline numbers
| Situation | Contact |
|---|---|
| Danger right now | 112 |
| Domestic violence, need shelter, counselling or referral | 181 or the nearest One Stop Centre |
| Online harassment, leaked images, fake profile | cybercrime.gov.in |
| Harassment at work | The workplace's ICC or the district LCC; SHe-Box portal |
| Cannot afford a lawyer | 15100 or the District Legal Services Authority |
| Want to complain to a national body | National Commission for Women (ncw.gov.in) |
