# IT Act, 2000 — privacy violation and obscene or sexually explicit material online

## Section 66E — Punishment for violation of privacy
- source: IT Act, Section 66E | topic: violation_of_privacy | keywords: camera, capture, publish, transmit, image, private area, naked, pictures, photos, consent, deepfake, hidden camera, voyeurism
Whoever intentionally or knowingly captures, publishes or transmits the image of a private area of any person without his or her consent, under circumstances violating the privacy of that person, shall be punished with imprisonment which may extend to three years or with fine not exceeding two lakh rupees, or with both.
- Punishment: imprisonment up to 3 years, or fine up to Rs. 2,00,000, or both.
- Applies to capturing, publishing or transmitting. Consent is the key test.

## Section 66E (Explanation) — Meaning of "private area", "publishes" and "under circumstances violating privacy"
- source: IT Act, Section 66E Explanation | topic: violation_of_privacy | status: supplementary, verify against India Code | keywords: private area, publish, reasonable expectation of privacy, naked, undergarments, breast, buttocks
- "Private area" means the naked or undergarment-clad genitals, pubic area, buttocks or female breast.
- "Publishes" means reproduction in printed or electronic form and making it available for public.
- "Under circumstances violating privacy" means circumstances in which a person can have a reasonable expectation that he or she could disrobe in privacy without being concerned that an image of his or her private area was being captured, or that any part of his or her private area would not be visible to the public, regardless of whether the person is in a public or private place.

## Section 67 — Publishing or transmitting obscene material in electronic form
- source: IT Act, Section 67 | topic: obscene_electronic_material | keywords: obscene, lascivious, deprave, corrupt, internet, whatsapp, social media, messages, videos
Whoever publishes or transmits or causes to be published or transmitted in electronic form any material which is lascivious or appeals to the prurient interest, or whose effect is such as to tend to deprave and corrupt persons who are likely, having regard to all relevant circumstances, to read, see or hear the matter contained or embodied in it, shall be punished:
- First conviction: imprisonment of either description up to 3 years and fine up to Rs. 5,00,000.
- Second or subsequent conviction: imprisonment of either description up to 5 years and fine up to Rs. 10,00,000.

## Section 67A — Publishing or transmitting material containing sexually explicit act
- source: IT Act, Section 67A | topic: sexually_explicit_material | keywords: sexually explicit, sex act, porn, morphed, revenge porn, videos, internet, blackmail
Whoever publishes or transmits or causes to be published or transmitted in electronic form any material which contains a sexually explicit act or conduct shall be punished:
- First conviction: imprisonment of either description up to 5 years and fine up to Rs. 10,00,000.
- Second or subsequent conviction: imprisonment of either description up to 7 years and fine up to Rs. 10,00,000.

## Section 67B — Child sexual abuse material in electronic form
- source: IT Act, Section 67B | topic: child_sexual_abuse_material | status: supplementary, verify against India Code | keywords: child, minor, under 18, CSAM, child pornography, grooming online, browsing, downloading
Publishing, transmitting, creating, collecting, browsing, downloading, advertising, promoting or exchanging electronic material depicting children in a sexually explicit act or conduct is an offence, as is inducing or enticing children into an online relationship for sexually explicit acts, or recording abuse of children.
- First conviction: imprisonment of either description up to 5 years and fine up to Rs. 10,00,000.
- Second or subsequent conviction: imprisonment of either description up to 7 years and fine up to Rs. 10,00,000.

## Section 66C — Identity theft
- source: IT Act, Section 66C | topic: identity_theft | status: supplementary, verify against India Code | keywords: fake profile, password, impersonation, account misuse, deepfake, identity
Whoever fraudulently or dishonestly makes use of the electronic signature, password or any other unique identification feature of any other person shall be punished with imprisonment of either description up to 3 years and fine up to Rs. 1,00,000.

## Section 66D — Cheating by personation using a computer resource
- source: IT Act, Section 66D | topic: online_personation | status: supplementary, verify against India Code | keywords: fake profile, impersonate, cheating, personation, deepfake, online fraud
Whoever, by means of any communication device or computer resource, cheats by personation shall be punished with imprisonment of either description up to 3 years and fine up to Rs. 1,00,000.
