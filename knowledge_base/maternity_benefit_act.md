# Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (Amended 2017) — leave, pay, crèche and job protection

## Section 5 — Right to payment of maternity benefit
- source: Maternity Benefit Act, Section 5(1), 5(3) as amended | topic: maternity_leave_duration | keywords: pregnancy, leave, 26 weeks, salary, payment, average daily wage, two children, 12 weeks, before delivery
Every woman is entitled to, and her employer is liable for, payment of maternity benefit at the rate of the average daily wage for the period of her actual absence.
- Maximum period: 26 weeks, of which not more than 8 weeks may precede the expected date of delivery.
- Two or more surviving children: maximum 12 weeks, of which not more than 6 weeks may precede the expected date of delivery.

## Section 5(2) — Eligibility: 80 days of work
- source: Maternity Benefit Act, Section 5(2) | topic: maternity_eligibility | status: supplementary, verify against India Code | keywords: eligibility, 80 days, who qualifies, probation, new employee
A woman is entitled to maternity benefit only if she has actually worked in the establishment of the employer from whom she claims it for a period of not less than 80 days in the 12 months immediately preceding the date of her expected delivery.

## Section 5(4) — Adopting mother and commissioning mother
- source: Maternity Benefit Act, Section 5(4); Amendment Act 2017, Section 3(B) | topic: adoption_surrogacy_leave | keywords: adopting, adoption, commissioning mother, surrogacy, 12 weeks, child handed over, three months
A woman who legally adopts a child below the age of three months, or a commissioning mother, is entitled to maternity benefit for 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over to the adopting mother or commissioning mother, as the case may be.

## Section 3(ba) — Definition of "commissioning mother"
- source: Amendment Act 2017, Section 2 (inserts Section 3(ba)) | topic: definitions | keywords: commissioning mother, surrogacy, egg, embryo, definition
"Commissioning mother" means a biological mother who uses her egg to create an embryo implanted in any other woman.

## Section 5(5) — Work from home after maternity benefit
- source: Maternity Benefit Act, Section 5(5); Amendment Act 2017, Section 3(B) | topic: work_from_home | keywords: work from home, remote work, after leave, mutual agreement
Where the nature of work assigned to a woman is such that she may work from home, the employer may allow her to do so after she has availed of the maternity benefit, for such period and on such conditions as the employer and the woman mutually agree. This is at the employer's discretion and depends on the nature of the work; it is not an automatic right.

## Section 9 — Leave for miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy
- source: Maternity Benefit Act, Section 9 | topic: miscarriage_leave | status: supplementary, verify against India Code | keywords: miscarriage, abortion, medical termination of pregnancy, MTP, six weeks leave
A woman who has a miscarriage or a medical termination of pregnancy is entitled, on production of the prescribed proof, to leave with wages at the rate of maternity benefit for 6 weeks immediately following the day of the miscarriage or medical termination.

## Section 11A — Crèche facility
- source: Maternity Benefit Act, Section 11A; Amendment Act 2017, Section 4 | topic: creche_and_childcare | keywords: creche, daycare, 50 employees, child care, visits, rest interval, nursing, benefits notice, initial appointment
- Every establishment having 50 or more employees must have a crèche facility within the prescribed distance, either separately or along with common facilities.
- The employer must allow four visits a day to the crèche by the woman, which includes the interval for rest allowed to her.
- Every establishment must tell every woman, in writing and electronically, at the time of her initial appointment, about every benefit available under the Act.

## Section 12 — Dismissal during absence or pregnancy
- source: Maternity Benefit Act, Section 12 | topic: protection_against_dismissal | keywords: fired, terminated, discharged, pregnancy, maternity leave, notice, job loss, illegal termination, change of service conditions
When a woman is absent from work in accordance with the Act, it is unlawful for her employer to:
- discharge or dismiss her during or on account of such absence;
- give notice of discharge or dismissal on a day such that the notice expires during her absence; or
- vary to her disadvantage any of the conditions of her service.

## Commencement of the 2017 amendments
- source: MoLE Notification S.O. 1026(E), 31 March 2017, as corrected by Corrigendum S.O. 1049(E), 3 April 2017 | topic: effective_dates | keywords: when in force, effective date, 1 April 2017, 1 July 2017, commencement, creche effective date
- The Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017 received Presidential assent on 27 March 2017.
- Most provisions (including the 26-week leave, adoption and commissioning mother benefits and the definition of commissioning mother) came into force on 1 April 2017.
- The crèche provision (Amendment Act Section 4(1), inserting Section 11A(1)) came into force on 1 July 2017.
- The original notification wrongly referred to "sub-section (5) of section 3" for the July date; the corrigendum corrected both references to "sub-section (1) of section 4".

## Quick reference — leave entitlements under the Act
