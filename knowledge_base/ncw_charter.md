# NCW Citizen's Charter 2026-2027

## Role of NCW — what it does and what it does not do
- org: NCW | topic: authority_scope | keywords: what can NCW do, is NCW a court, who enforces, implementing agency
NCW's vision is the Indian woman, secure in her home and outside, fully empowered to access all her rights and entitlements. Its mission is to enable women to achieve equality and equal participation by securing their rights through policy formulation, legislative measures, effective enforcement of laws, implementation of schemes and strategies for problems arising from discrimination and atrocities against women.
The charter states clearly that NCW is not an implementing agency. In practice it examines safeguards, looks into complaints, takes suo motu notice, and takes matters up with the appropriate authorities; the charter does not describe NCW as prosecuting offences or passing orders in the way a court does.

## Functions of the Commission (Section 10 of the NCW Act)
- org: NCW | source_ref: NCW Act s.10, as listed in the charter | topic: authority_scope | keywords: NCW functions, suo motu, investigate, inspect jails, fund litigation
The Commission: (a) investigates and examines matters relating to safeguards for women under the Constitution and other laws; (b) presents annual and other reports to the Central Government on how those safeguards work; (c) recommends effective implementation of safeguards; (d) reviews existing laws affecting women and recommends amendments; (e) takes up violations of constitutional and legal provisions relating to women with appropriate authorities; (f) looks into complaints and takes suo motu notice of deprivation of women's rights, non-implementation of laws protecting women, and non-compliance with policy decisions, guidelines or instructions meant to relieve hardship and ensure women's welfare, and takes these up with appropriate authorities; (g) calls for special studies or investigations into discrimination and atrocities against women; (h) undertakes promotional and educational research; (i) participates and advises in socio-economic planning for women; (j) evaluates progress of women's development; (k) inspects jails, remand homes, women's institutions and other places of custody where women are kept and takes up remedial action; (l) funds litigation involving issues affecting a large body of women; (m) makes periodic reports to the Government; (n) handles any other matter referred by the Government.

## Who NCW serves
- org: NCW | topic: stakeholders | keywords: clients, stakeholders, who can approach NCW
All citizens, particularly women facing domestic violence, molestation or workplace harassment; Central ministries and departments; State Women Commissions, State Governments and UT administrations; district and local administrations; NGOs and women's rights activists; civil society organisations; universities and research organisations; and independent legal and other experts.

## NCW services and service standards
- org: NCW | topic: services | keywords: services, grievance handling, legal safeguards, monitoring
Services include studying and monitoring legal safeguards for women, addressing unequal status through policy recommendations, facilitating redress of grievances by looking into complaints and taking suo motu notice (including deprivation of women's rights, to provide support legal or otherwise, and non-compliance with welfare and relief measures), and advising on socio-economic planning and evaluating progress. Service standards include investigating and examining legal safeguards, reviewing laws for gaps, looking into complaints and taking them up with authorities, studies and research, and inspecting places of custody. The charter says grievances received in the Commission receive instant attention, without stating a time limit.

## What NCW expects from the public
- org: NCW | topic: expectations
The charter asks citizens and organisations to interact with the Commission to improve its efficiency, promote the safety of women, follow the rules framed by the Government for women's protection, share information with other citizens on protection of women, and adopt the precepts of protecting women's rights.
